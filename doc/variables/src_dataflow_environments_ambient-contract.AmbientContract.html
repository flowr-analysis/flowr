    The ambient contracts of functions: what a call does to the global state, with the conditions of AmbientEffect#when and AmbientEffect#unless as data to evaluate rather than words to parse, and narrowed to what the arguments of one call decide. map(add = TRUE) of maps adds to the plot and starts none, map(...) in a function forwarding its arguments may do either.

      • ofCall(
            this: void,
            call: NodeId,
            info: ResolveInfo,
            sites?: readonly CallSite<ParentInformation>[],
        ): readonly CallAmbientContract[]

        The ambient contracts of the call call of a finished analysis, one per built-in definition the call may reach, as map(...) may call maps::map as well as purrr::map, which do different things to the global state. A definition flowR states no effects for has no contract. The contracts are interprocedural: a call of a function the program defines has the contracts of the calls its body makes, narrowed to what the call gives (g <- function(...) maps::map(...) and g(add = TRUE) adds to the plot), and a call handing on parameters (maps::map(...) within g) is narrowed to what the known calls of its function give, see CallSites.contexts. The sites are the calls the body of call is reached through, innermost first.
