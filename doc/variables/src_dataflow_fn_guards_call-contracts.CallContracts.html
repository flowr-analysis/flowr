    The one layer to ask what a function asks of its calls, its contract, and whether a call keeps it: stated by the signature database or flowR's tables for a function by name, or inferred from a definition of the program by the guards analysis, see FunctionGuards. The conditions of the guards of the callee are taken to the call as the guard analysis takes them to a caller, and a call only breaks a condition if it fails for every value the arguments may hold.
